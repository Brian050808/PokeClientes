okeClientes lets you keep a simple list of clients on your device.

- Create a local account and sign in with email and password.
- Add, edit and delete clients with name, phone and email.
- Create your own classifications and assign them to clients.
- Browse your phone contacts and open a WhatsApp chat from them.
- Scan QR codes and open their links.
- Browse Pokémon data loaded from PokeAPI (requires internet).

Your clients and account are stored only on your device. No Google services, ads or trackers.
